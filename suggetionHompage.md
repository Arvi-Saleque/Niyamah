My recommended homepage concept
“Smart Shopping Command Center”

Do not make a normal homepage.

Make it like a premium shopping dashboard where the customer instantly sees:

what is new
what is trending
what is on offer
what category they need
what products match their intent
how to order
why they can trust the store
where to get help

This is the best direction for your site.

Homepage structure I recommend
1. Sticky smart header

The header should not be basic.

It should contain:

Logo | Mega Menu | Search Bar | Campaign Badge | Wishlist | Cart | Account

On mobile:

Logo | Search Icon | Cart | Menu

But when scrolling, show a compact sticky search bar:

Search products, brands, categories...

This is important because search is a major product-finding path in e-commerce. Baymard’s 2026 search benchmark says 56% of sites fail to support users’ search needs properly, so search should become one of your strongest UI features.

Unique header idea

Add a small animated line under the header:

Free delivery over ৳2000  •  COD available  •  7-day return  •  WhatsApp support

This gives trust immediately.

2. Hero section should become interactive

Your current homepage hero is too simple.

Instead, use a full-screen editorial hero slider.

Hero layout

Left side:

Premium Collection
Elegant essentials for everyday style
Shop new arrivals

Right side:

product image
floating price card
offer badge
review badge
quick category chips

Example:

[New Arrival]
Niyamah Signature Collection

Soft, elegant, curated products for modern lifestyle.

[Shop Collection] [View Offers]

Popular:
Women | Gifts | Home | New Drop
Unique interaction

Inside the hero, add a mini “shopping intent selector”:

I am shopping for:
[Myself] [Gift] [Home] [Offer] [New Arrival]

When user clicks Gift, the homepage changes sections to gift collections.

When user clicks Offer, sale products become more visible.

This is not hard technically, but feels very premium.

3. Smart search block below hero

Do not keep search only in the header.

Add a beautiful search discovery panel:

What are you looking for today?

[ Search for dress, gift, skincare, home decor... ]

Trending searches:
Eid outfit | Gift under ৳1000 | New arrivals | Best sellers

Autocomplete should show:

product suggestions
category suggestions
brand suggestions
popular searches
thumbnail images

Baymard says autocomplete exists on many e-commerce sites, but only 19% get all implementation details right, so a polished autocomplete can make your store feel much more professional.

Most sites show:

Categories

You should show both:

Shop by Category
Fashion | Beauty | Home | Accessories | Gifts

This is much more customer-friendly because many users do not know the exact category. They know their situation.

NN/g says e-commerce homepages and navigation should help customers understand what the site sells, find products, and decide what to buy.

5. Personalized “For You” section

Add a section like:

Recommended For You
Based on your browsing, wishlist, and trending products

At first, before you have enough user data, use:

trending products
best sellers
recently viewed
category-based recommendation
new arrivals

Later, make it AI-powered.

Shopify’s 2026 personalization guide explains that personalization can include product recommendations, offers, search results, and support based on customer behavior and first-party data.

UI idea

Use tabs:

For You | Trending | New Arrival | Best Seller | Recently Viewed

This makes the homepage feel alive.

6. Campaign section should be bold

Your homepage must have a strong offer/campaign area.

Example:

Eid Premium Drop
Up to 30% off selected products
Ends in 02d : 14h : 22m

[Explore Campaign]

Design style:

big image
countdown timer
product preview cards
floating discount badge
progress bar: “64% stock claimed”

This creates urgency without looking cheap.

7. Product sections should not be repetitive

Do not show only one product grid.

Use different product layouts.

Section 1: New Arrivals

Clean product grid.

Section 2: Best Sellers

Horizontal scroll cards.

Section 3: Editor’s Picks

Premium editorial cards.

Section 4: Under Budget

Budget chips:

Under ৳499 | Under ৳999 | Under ৳1499 | Premium
Section 5: Recently Viewed

Only show when available.

This gives variety and keeps the customer engaged.

8. Add “AI Shopping Assistant” section

This can become your most unique feature.

Section title:

Not sure what to buy?
Let Niyamah Assistant help you.

Options:

Find a gift
Build my outfit
Find products under my budget
Compare products
Ask about delivery

UI:

[ I need a gift for my sister under ৳1500 ]
[ Find products ]

This should open a chat drawer, not a separate page.

For Bangladesh market, also connect this with WhatsApp:

Continue on WhatsApp

This can become one of your biggest selling points as a SaaS template.

9. Trust section must appear early

Do not put trust only in footer.

Add after first product/campaign area:

Why customers trust us

Cash on Delivery
Fast Bangladesh Delivery
Easy Return Policy
Verified Reviews
Secure Checkout
WhatsApp Support

Make each trust item clickable.

Example:

Click “Cash on Delivery” → opens small modal explaining COD.
Click “Return Policy” → opens return summary.
Click “Delivery” → opens district-based delivery info.

This gives confidence before checkout.

10. Add “Live shopping proof”

This makes the site feel active.

Example:

Someone from Dhaka ordered 12 minutes ago

Use softly, not aggressively.

Design:

small floating toast
bottom-left

can be disabled

This creates a premium live-commerce feeling.

13. Add review and social proof section

Not just normal testimonials.

Make it more interactive:

This helps trust and conversion.
